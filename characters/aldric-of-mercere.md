# Aldric of Mercere
## The Warband's Aura — Enhancement Battle Mage

> **Draft Character — Please Review Choices Below**

---

## Concept

Aldric is a large, battle-hardened Gifted Redcap of House Mercere who has fused Hermetic discipline with the life of a soldier. Where destructive magi hurl fire and ruin, Aldric walks *into* the fight alongside his companions — and more crucially, he makes **them** deadlier. Before any engagement, he moves through his allies like a general before the charge: touching each fighter, suffusing their flesh with golden warmth, tuning their muscles and reflexes to superhuman precision. In battle, their blades seem guided; their strikes find gaps in armour that should not exist.

Aldric himself is no bystander. He wades into the melee in chain mail, longsword and shield, his own body wrapped in hardened flesh and inexhaustible endurance. He is the rallying point — the last to fall, the first to give his warriors the edge they need.

His magic is **Creo Corpus** (creating/perfecting the human body) backed by **Muto Corpus** (transforming the body for temporary enhancement). He can permanently raise a warrior's natural Dexterity to a higher peak (a rare and permanent gift), or invest a whole group with sharper coordination before a battle. His weaknesses are equally defining: he cannot destroy or harm through magic, and his compassion is a constant tactical liability.

## Spell Design Notes

### Aura of Martial Precision (MuCo 30) — The Core "To Hit" Buff

> **This is the character's signature combat enhancement.** It provides the "heavy bonus to hit" requested by touching one member of a group and letting the effect wash through all of them.

**Design:**
- Base 5 (Muto Corpus guideline: *make a body resistant to damage +1 Soak* — used here as equivalent physical combat bonus)
- Interpreted as: temporarily altering the musculature, reflexes, and kinaesthetic sense of the targets to give them +2 to all Attack Totals
- Range Touch (+1 magnitude, +5 levels)
- Duration Sun (+2 magnitudes, +10 levels)
- Target Group (+2 magnitudes, +10 levels)

`Base 5 + 5 (Touch) + 10 (Sun) + 10 (Group) = **Level 30**`

**Casting Total:** Mu(7) + Co(12) + Focus(+7) + Sta(2) = **28 + die** — a roll of 2+ succeeds, making this very reliable.

**Effect in Play:** +2 to Attack Total for every member of the affected group for the whole day. For a group of 5 warriors with a typical Attack of +10, they now attack at +12 — enough to change outcomes decisively against most opponents.

**Storyguide Note:** The +2 Attack bonus is derived from the Muto Corpus "minor physical ability" guideline. If your troupe prefers a stricter reading, this could alternatively be framed as raising each target's Dexterity by 1 for Sun duration (using a Muto Corpus guideline for temporary characteristic change, agreed with the storyguide). The effect is equivalent.

### Blessing of the Steadfast Champion (CrCo 45, Ritual) — Permanent To-Hit Enhancement

> **The ultimate lasting gift:** Aldric can permanently raise a valued warrior's natural Dexterity to a higher peak. A warrior blessed this way will be a better fighter for the rest of their life.

**Design:**
- Guideline Level 40: *Increase one of a person's physical Characteristics by one point, to no more than +2*
- Range Touch (+1 magnitude, +5 levels)
- Duration Momentary (Ritual — permanent change, as per CrCo healing conventions)
- Target Individual

`Base 40 + 5 (Touch) = **Level 45 Ritual**`

- Requires **9 pawns of Corpus or Creo vis**
- Takes a full **season** to perform
- **Effect:** A warrior with Dex 0 becomes Dex +1, permanently adding +1 to their Attack Total for all future combats
- Aldric's CrCo Lab Total is **+50**, so he can invent this in a single season

**Storyguide Note:** This ritual represents Aldric's most treasured gift to a companion. He might perform it as a rite of brotherhood before a critical campaign. The vis cost is significant and limits how often it can be given.

### Shield of the Iron Body (MuCo 30) — Allied Soak Buff

**Design:**

## Appearance & Backstory

Aldric stands a head taller than most men — not monstrous, but notably large, with the broad shoulders and weathered hands of someone who spent as much time at the training post as at the desk. His hair is dark, cut short in the Norman fashion. The Gift gives his amber eyes an unsettling quality; people around him feel vaguely on edge, as if something important has been left unsaid.

He was born to a minor Norman noble family. At ten, a passing magus of Mercere recognised the Gift in the boy and took him as an apprentice — something that caused Aldric's family considerable relief. His Gift had been disrupting livestock and servants for months.

His parens, a Gifted Redcap named Constance of Mercere, was a traveller and healer, and she shaped his arts around the body and its improvement. Aldric applied this toward a different end: where she healed the sick, he fortified the strong. By the time of his Gauntlet he had already won three duels against grog escorts using longsword and shield, a fact his parens found amusing and slightly concerning.

In the five years since his Gauntlet he has accompanied two military expeditions as a covenant representative, lending his enhancement magic to the grogs and knights who protected his sodales. The experience solidified his identity: he is not a wizard who is occasionally also a fighter, but a warrior-mage who chooses to stand in the line.

His wizard's sigil: a faint warmth, like the first light of morning, that suffuses any target of his magic.

## Design Choices — Explanation for Review

### Why House Mercere?
House Mercere grants **Puissant Creo or Puissant Muto** as a free house benefit. Puissant Creo is ideal here: it permanently raises his effective Creo score by 2 for **all purposes** (casting, Lab Totals, determining maximum learnable spell level). For a Creo-specialist enhancement mage, this is the best possible house fit. It also gives him a narrative hook as a Gifted Redcap — a rarer and somewhat prestigious position within the House.

### Why Minor Magical Focus: Enhancing the Human Body?
The Focus applies to both **CrCo** (permanent improvement, rituals) and **MuCo** (temporary enhancement, battle buffs), as both are narrower subsets of those two TeFo combinations. This doubles the lower Art in every relevant Lab Total and casting total — giving him a combined **+12 to CrCo casting** and **+7 to MuCo casting** on top of everything else. It's what gives him the ability to invent Level 45 rituals so early.

### Why Warrior?
Magi may learn Martial Abilities during and after apprenticeship without a virtue, but **only during apprenticeship** — not during early childhood or later life. The Warrior virtue lets him arrive at apprenticeship with **Single Weapon 4** (50 bonus XP), meaning he is already a skilled swordsman from youth, not a mage who reluctantly picked up a sword later. It shapes his background authentically.

### Why Deficient Perdo?
This is the Hermetic flaw (required for all magi) and it reinforces the concept beautifully: Aldric literally cannot harm through magic effectively. His magic creates, improves, endures. The flaw costs him nothing thematically and everything mechanically in a destructive role — exactly what we want.

### The Attack Total in Play

A group of 6 warriors with average Attack +9 (Dex 0, Combat Ability 5, Weapon +4):

| State | Attack Total |
|---|---|
| Baseline | +9 |
| After *Aura of Martial Precision* | **+11** (+2 bonus) |
| After *Aura* + *Precision of the Hawk* on leader | **+14** (+5 bonus on vanguard) |
| After *Blessing of the Steadfast Champion* (permanent) | **+12** (+1 permanent Dex) |

Combined with **Endurance of the Berserkers** on each key fighter (ignoring wound penalties), the group maintains peak attack effectiveness even while taking wounds — which is typically when combats turn against a group.

### Areas for Troupe Discussion
1. **Aura of Martial Precision**: The +2 Attack bonus interpretation from MuCo base 5 is a custom ruling. Your troupe may prefer to treat this as *"temporary Dexterity boost of +2"* — the level would be the same and the effect equivalent, but the framing matches the Creo Corpus characteristic guidelines.
2. **Does the Focus cover both CrCo and MuCo?** The focus description as *"Enhancing the Human Body"* is written to span both, similar to how *"Healing"* spans CrCo and CrAn. If the troupe prefers a tighter reading, restrict the focus to CrCo only, which lowers MuCo casting and Lab Totals but leaves his core CrCo ritual ability unchanged.
3. **Post-Gauntlet spell inventions**: The 4 lab seasons and XP allocation approximate 150 XP; small adjustments may be needed to satisfy exact XP accounting.
