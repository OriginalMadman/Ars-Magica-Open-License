### How to read this grid

**Every cell is a Casting Score, not a finished roll.** The Casting Score is the fixed part of
the total; the dice and the situational modifiers go on top of it:

> **Casting Score = Technique + Form + Stamina − Encumbrance + Aura Modifier**
>
> **Formulaic Casting Total = Casting Score + die** — a simple die when relaxed, a stress die under pressure

Then compare the Casting Total to the spell's level:

| Casting Total − Spell Level | Spell cast? | Fatigue lost |
|---|---|---|
| 0 or higher | Yes | none |
| −1 to −10 | Yes | 1 level |
| −11 or lower | No | 1 level |

So a cell of 31 means Aldric casts any Creo Corpus spell of level 31 or less with no die roll
needed and no cost, and anything up to level 41 at the price of a Fatigue level.

### Modifiers to add to a cell

| Modifier | Value | When |
|---|---:|---|
| **Magical Focus** | **+12** Creo Corpus, **+7** Muto Corpus | Spells that improve a person's Characteristics. Add the *lower* of Technique and Form a second time. |
| **Method Caster** | **+3** | Any Formulaic or Ritual spell — but only if he casts by the book. Vary the gestures or the voicing and it is gone. |
| **Encumbrance** | **−2** | In full harness (chain mail 6 + longsword 1 + heater shield 2 = Load 9 → Burden 3 − Str 1). Zero when he is out of armour. Encumbrance hits casting and Initiative; it does **not** touch Attack or Defense. |
| **Aura** | **+3** typical covenant, **0** in the field, negative in a Divine or Infernal aura | The grid assumes 0, i.e. on campaign. Casting at home is three points easier. |
| **Spell Mastery** | +Mastery score | For a mastered spell; also removes that many botch dice. |
| **Raw vis** | +2 per pawn, up to his score in that Art | Also **+1 botch die per pawn**. |
| **Words and gestures** | Loud +1, firm 0, quiet −5, none −10 · Exaggerated +1, bold 0, subtle −2, none −5 | A Ritual cannot be cast at all without a loud voice and exaggerated gestures. |
| **Confidence** | +3 | Three points to spend per session. |
| Wound and Fatigue penalties | −1 / −3 / −5 | Cumulative, and they apply to casting as much as to swinging. |

### Why everything except the Ritual stops at level 25

> "Anyone subjected to a powerful supernatural effect gains a Warping Point, unless they
> themselves were responsible for the effect or it was specifically and carefully designed to
> work on them. 'Powerful effect' is subjective, but **any Hermetic spell of sixth magnitude
> or higher counts.**"

Sixth magnitude is level 26. A buff-mage who habitually casts level 30 spells on the same
grogs Warps his own warband — Warping Score 1 arrives after five points, Score 2 after
fifteen. **Level 25 is the ceiling for anything Aldric casts on someone else.** It is the
single constraint that shapes this spell list, and it is why *Aegis of Hardened Flesh* takes
Diameter duration (two minutes — twenty combat rounds, ample for a melee) rather than Sun.

*Blessing of the Steadfast Champion* deliberately breaks the rule: at magnitude 9 it costs its
recipient one Warping Point, once. That is a fair price for a permanent gift, and Aldric can
remove even that by inventing a version tailored to a named man — a spell "specifically and
carefully designed to work on them" Warps nobody.

### Two rulings to settle with the troupe

1. **The attack buff's guideline.** There is no Muto Corpus guideline for a combat bonus. These
   spells use base 2, *"Change someone to give them a minor ability"* — read as sharpened
   reflexes and an unerring sense of an opponent's guard, worth +1 Attack. The alternative
   reading is Creo Corpus temporary Characteristic increase (base 35 for Dexterity to +1),
   which puts a Touch/Sun/Group version at level 60 and out of reach. Agree which before play.
2. **Focus breadth.** Written here as *improving the body's Characteristics*, which is narrow
   enough to read as Minor and still covers every spell above. "Enhancing the human body"
   covers most of two whole Technique/Form combinations and is arguably Major-focus breadth.

## Combat scores

| Score | Total | Built from |
|---|---:|---|
| **Initiative** | **+1** | Qik +1 + weapon Init +2 − Encumbrance 2 |
| **Attack** | **+13** | Dex +2 + Single Weapon 7 + weapon Atk +4 |
| **Defense** | **+12** | Qik +1 + Single Weapon 7 + weapon Dfn +1 + shield Dfn +3 |
| **Damage** | **+7** | Str +1 + weapon Dam +6 **+ Attack Advantage** |
| **Soak** | **+14** | Sta +2 + chain mail 9 + Tough 3 |

Add a stress die to Initiative, Attack and Defense. **Soak takes no die in melee** — it is a
flat, reliable number. **Attack Advantage (Attack Total − opponent's Defense Total) is added
straight to the Damage Total**, which is why a +1 Attack buff is also a +1 damage buff.

Against missiles, only the shield's +3 adds to Defense; his Single Weapon score still applies
as dodging.

## Self-buffed

| Buff | Effect |
|---|---|
| *Gift of the Bear's Fortitude* (MuCo 25, Per/Sun) | Soak **+17**; −1 to anything requiring a delicate touch |
| *Endurance of the Berserkers* (ReCo 15, Per/Conc) | Ignores all wound and Fatigue penalties while he concentrates. Wounds can still worsen without his noticing, and the Fatigue all falls due the moment it ends |

At Soak 17, a competent man-at-arms doing +7 damage needs an Attack Advantage of **11** — that
is, to beat Aldric's Defense Total of 12 + die by eleven points — before he raises a bruise.

**Both self-buffs are Range: Personal, and that is deliberate.** Personal is the only Range
that skips the caster's own Magic Resistance; a Touch-range spell cast on himself would have
to penetrate Corpus 12 + Parma 5 = 17. Anything he cannot build at Personal range, he casts
*before* performing the Parma Magica ritual.

## Pre-battle routine

1. **At the covenant, out of armour** (Encumbrance 0, Aura +3 — worth 5 points on every total):
   cast the Moon-duration buffs — *Aura of Martial Precision* on the turb, *Precision of the
   Hawk* on the vanguard. They last a month; march out already buffed.
2. **Arm.** Encumbrance 2 costs him casting and Initiative but nothing on Attack or Defense.
3. **Cast *Gift of the Bear's Fortitude*** (Sun duration), then perform **Parma Magica**. In
   that order, so his own resistance is down while he buffs himself.
4. **On contact:** *Aegis of Hardened Flesh* and *Hide of the Whole Warband* — Diameter
   duration, twenty rounds, cast as the enemy closes.
5. **In the line:** *Endurance of the Berserkers* when he takes his first serious wound.
6. **After:** *Rally of the Fallen* over the wounded, then *Bind Wound* on anyone who cannot
   be moved.

Note that grogs and mundane soldiers have no Magic Resistance at all, so every buff lands on
them automatically regardless of Penetration. Buffing a fellow magus is a different matter and
needs Penetration Total = Casting Total + 2 − spell level to beat their Form + 5 × Parma.

## Appearance and Background

Aldric stands a head taller than most men, broad-shouldered, with the weathered hands of
someone who spent as much time at the training post as at the desk. Dark hair cut short in the
Norman fashion. The Gift gives his amber eyes an unsettling quality; people near him feel
vaguely on edge, as though something important has been left unsaid.

Born to a minor Norman noble family. At ten a passing magus of Mercere recognised the Gift in
the boy and took him as an apprentice, to his family's considerable relief — his Gift had been
troubling the livestock and the servants for months. His parens, a Gifted Redcap named
Constance of Mercere, was a traveller and a healer, and shaped his Arts around the body and
its improvement. Aldric turned that to a different end: where she mended the sick, he fortifies
the strong. By his Gauntlet he had won three duels against grog escorts with sword and shield,
which his parens found amusing and slightly concerning.

In the five years since, he has accompanied two military expeditions as a covenant
representative, lending his magic to the grogs and knights who guarded his sodales. He is not a
wizard who occasionally fights. He is a warrior who happens to be a magus, and who has worked
out that the most useful thing he can do in a battle is make the men beside him better at it.

What he owes House Mercere for the privilege of that freedom is a debt nobody has yet named
aloud.
