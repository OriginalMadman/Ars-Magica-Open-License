**On the Magical Focus.** *Bodily enhancement* is a slice of Creo Corpus and a slice of Muto
Corpus — the same shape as the rulebook's own example of *healing*, which spans Creo Corpus,
Creo Animal and possibly Creo Herbam. It is narrower than either combination: Creo Corpus also
covers healing, ageing and creation, and Muto Corpus also covers shapechanging, appearance and
transformation into animals or objects. The focus therefore applies to the Soak buffs, the
attack buffs and the Characteristic ritual — but **not** to his healing and recovery magic,
which uses the plain Creo Corpus totals.

### How to read the grid

**A cell is a Casting Score, not a finished roll.** It is the fixed part of the total; the dice
and the situational modifiers go on top of it.

> **Casting Score = Technique + Form + Stamina − Encumbrance + Aura Modifier**
>
> **Formulaic Casting Total = Casting Score + die** — a simple die when relaxed, a stress die under pressure

Then compare that total to the spell's level:

| Casting Total − Spell Level | Spell cast? | Fatigue lost |
|---|---|---|
| 0 or higher | Yes | none |
| −1 to −10 | Yes | one level |
| −11 or lower | No | one level |

So the Creo Corpus cell of **31** means Aldric casts any Creo Corpus spell of level 31 or less
with no roll needed and no cost, and anything up to level 41 at the price of a Fatigue level —
before the focus, which for enhancement spells adds twelve more.

### Modifiers to apply to a cell

| Modifier | Value | When it applies |
|---|---:|---|
| **Magical Focus** | **+12** Creo Corpus · **+7** Muto Corpus | Spells that enhance the body. Add the *lower* of Technique and Form a second time. Not his healing magic. |
| **Method Caster** | **+3** | Any Formulaic or Ritual spell — but only cast strictly by the book. Vary the gestures or the voicing and the bonus is gone. |
| **Encumbrance** | **−2** | In full harness. Zero out of armour. Encumbrance penalises casting and Initiative; it does **not** touch Attack or Defense. |
| **Aura** | **+3** at a typical covenant · **0** in the field · negative in a Divine or Infernal aura | The grid assumes 0, that is, on campaign. |
| **Spell Mastery** | + Mastery score | For a mastered spell; it also removes that many botch dice. |
| **Raw vis** | +2 per pawn, up to his score in that Art | And **+1 botch die per pawn**. |
| **Words and gestures** | Loud +1 · firm 0 · quiet −5 · none −10 <br> Exaggerated +1 · bold 0 · subtle −2 · none −5 | A Ritual cannot be cast at all without a loud voice and exaggerated gestures. |
| **Confidence** | +3 | Three points per session. |
| **Wounds and Fatigue** | −1 / −3 / −5 | Cumulative, and they hit casting as hard as they hit swordplay. |

### Why nothing he casts on other people exceeds level 25

Any Hermetic spell of **sixth magnitude or higher — level 26 and up — is a "powerful supernatural
effect"**, and anyone subjected to one gains a Warping Point unless they cast it themselves or it
was specially designed for them. A buff-mage who habitually casts level 30 spells on the same
grogs Warps his own warband: Warping Score 1 arrives after five points, Score 2 after fifteen.

**Level 25 is therefore the ceiling for anything Aldric casts on someone else.** It is the single
constraint that shapes this spell list, and it is why *Aegis of Hardened Flesh* takes Diameter
duration — two minutes, twenty combat rounds, ample for any melee — rather than Sun, which would
cost a magnitude more and push it over the line.

*Blessing of the Steadfast Champion* breaks the rule deliberately. At magnitude 9 it costs its
recipient a single Warping Point, once, which is a fair price for a permanent gift. Aldric can
remove even that by inventing a version tailored to a named man: a spell "specifically and
carefully designed to work on them" Warps nobody.

### The one ruling to settle with the troupe

There is no Muto Corpus guideline for a combat bonus. *Aura of Martial Precision* and *Precision
of the Hawk* use **base 2, "change someone to give them a minor ability"** — read as sharpened
reflexes and an unerring sense of an opponent's guard, worth **+1 Attack**. The alternative
reading is Creo Corpus temporary Characteristic increase, where base 35 raises a physical
Characteristic to no more than +1; a Touch / Sun / Group version of that is level 60, far beyond
a five-year magus. Agree which reading applies before play, because the whole concept rests on it.

# Combat

## Equipment

- **Longsword** — Init +2, Atk +4, Dfn +1, Dam +6, Str 0, Load 1, Expensive. A gift from his parens
- **Heater shield** — Dfn +3, Load 2
- **Full chain mail** — Protection 9, Load 6, Expensive
- Wizardly robes, a leather satchel holding 3 pawns of Corpus vis, and a sturdy Norman destrier

Weapon and shield modifiers add together. **Load 9 → Burden 3 → Encumbrance 2**, since
Encumbrance is the amount by which Burden exceeds Strength.

## Combat scores

| Score | Total | Built from |
|---|---:|---|
| **Initiative** | **+1** | Qik +1 + weapon Init +2 − Encumbrance 2 |
| **Attack** | **+13** | Dex +2 + Single Weapon 7 + weapon Atk +4 |
| **Defense** | **+12** | Qik +1 + Single Weapon 7 + weapon Dfn +1 + shield Dfn +3 |
| **Damage** | **+7** | Str +1 + weapon Dam +6, **plus the Attack Advantage** |
| **Soak** | **+14** | Sta +2 + chain mail 9 + Tough 3 |

Add a stress die to Initiative, Attack and Defense. **Soak takes no die in melee** — it is flat
and reliable. The **Attack Advantage** (Attack Total minus the opponent's Defense Total) is
added straight into the Damage Total, which is why an attack buff is also a damage buff.

Against missiles, only the shield's +3 adds to Defense, though his Single Weapon score still
counts as dodging.

## Wounds and Fatigue

**Wounds at Size +1:** Light 1–6 · Medium 7–12 · Heavy 13–18 · Incapacitating 19–24 · Dead 25+

Penalties −1 / −3 / −5, cumulative with Fatigue, applied to every action — **but never to Soak**.

**Fatigue:** Fresh 0 · Winded 0 · Weary −1 · Tired −3 · Dazed −5 · Unconscious

## Buffed for battle

| Buff | Effect |
|---|---|
| *Gift of the Bear's Fortitude* | Soak **+17**; −1 to anything requiring a delicate touch |
| *Aegis of Hardened Flesh* | The same +3 Soak, but at Touch range, so it is what he puts on somebody else. On himself *Bear's Fortitude* is strictly better: Personal range, and it lasts all day |
| *Endurance of the Berserkers* | Ignores every wound and Fatigue penalty while he concentrates. Wounds can still worsen without his noticing, and all that Fatigue falls due the instant it ends |

At Soak 17, a competent man-at-arms doing +7 damage needs an Attack Advantage of **11** — he must
beat Aldric's Defense Total of 12 plus a die by eleven points — before he raises a bruise.

Both of his standing self-buffs are **Range: Personal**, and that is deliberate. Personal is the
only Range that skips the caster's own Magic Resistance; a Touch-range spell cast on himself
would have to penetrate Corpus 12 + Parma 5 = 17. Anything he cannot build at Personal range, he
casts *before* performing the Parma Magica ritual.

**Caps observed.** At age 30 no Ability may exceed 6 at character creation; his highest are
French 5 and Single Weapon 5. Improved Characteristics does not lift the +3 ceiling on a single
Characteristic; Intelligence sits exactly on it. Every spell learned at Gauntlet is within the
maximum learnable level for its own Technique and Form. Spell levels above 5 are always multiples
of five, and below 5 a magnitude adds only one level — which is how *Eyes of the Cat* reaches 5
from base 2 and *Precision of the Hawk* reaches 10.

**A base Individual for Corpus is an adult human up to Size +1** — so Aldric is himself a base
Individual, with no size magnitudes needed. **A base Group is about ten of those**, which sets
the size of the warband he can cover with one casting.
